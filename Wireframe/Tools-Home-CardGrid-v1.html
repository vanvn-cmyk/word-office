Session 12 · Tools Home Redesign

Tools Home — 2-column Card Grid

Unify Organize + Fill & Sign + Convert into one card component pattern. 2 cards per row, 12pt spacing, ~116pt min-height. Convert keeps its direction indicator (2 badges + arrow). Others use single-icon variant. Scan hero stays as-is (attention grabber).

Card component: shared across all 3 sections. Icon badge top-left (32×32, 8pt radius, 12% tint bg). Title (SF 13pt semibold), subtitle (SF 11pt tertiary). Padding 14pt. Card radius 14pt, subtle 1pt border. Min-height 116pt for consistent row heights.

Grid math: 2 columns × 12pt gap. On a 393pt iPhone screen with 16pt side padding, each card = (393 − 32 − 12) / 2 = 174.5pt wide × ~116pt tall. Comfortable tap target, 4-word title fits on 2 lines max.

Convert direction indicator: 2 mini-badges (28×28) + arrow between them at the top of Convert cards. Preserves the "from → to" info that's the KEY piece of data for choosing a Convert direction. Non-Convert cards just have 1 icon.

Section counts: Convert 4 cards (2×2) · Organize 2 cards (1 row) · Fill & Sign 3 cards (2 + 1). The Fill & Sign 3-card odd count leaves one grid cell empty in the second row (Print sits left-aligned) — LazyVGrid natural behavior, doesn't look broken.

Decisions baked in (override any):

1. Scan hero stays — full-width card at top. Different visual weight from the grid below intentional (spec §6, primary attention grabber).

2. Section headers — moved from small icon+text to slightly bigger (SF 14pt semibold) with count badge on right. Better visual hierarchy for scan.

3. Card color — icon tint per document type (Word blue / PDF red / Image purple / Sign purple / Print blue), background neutral (dsBackgroundElevated). Card body doesn't compete with icon.

4. Convert direction indicator — kept 2-badge + arrow at top of Convert cards. The direction IS the key info for that tool; hiding it would force users to read titles more carefully.

5. Fill & Sign empty cell — LazyVGrid natural behavior: 3 items in 2-col = 2 top, 1 bottom-left, empty bottom-right. Doesn't look broken. If bothers user, alternative: 3-column row for Fill & Sign (cards narrower ~110pt wide) — call out.
